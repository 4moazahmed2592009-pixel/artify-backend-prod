Questions Before You Start

Does any Google account work? +
For image generation, your Google account must be 18+ years old. Family Link (supervised) accounts will not work due to AI provider policies.
Do I need to pay Google extra fees? +
No, the tool uses the free quota available to your own Google account. Your subscription with us covers the workspace, tools, consistency engine, and project management.
How are payments processed & refunds handled? +
Payments are securely processed via Gumroad. We do not store your card details. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee via Gumroad if you face unsolvable technical issues.
Are my generated images stored on your servers? +
We respect your privacy. We act as a smart intermediary; generations happen via Google servers and we do not store your scene images in our databases. Your projects are saved locally.

Important Account Notice

Please make sure your registered Google account date of birth is set to 18+ years of age. If not, switch to an adult account to avoid AI service interruptions.
